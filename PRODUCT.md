## Users

- **Primary Users (Makers)**: AICTE Scrutiny Officers in central and regional bureau offices during peak institutional approval seasons. Their primary job is triaging incoming Fixed Deposit Receipt (FDR) pledges, verifying OCR-extracted deposit attributes against college claims, inspecting physical and digital forgery signals on forensic canvases, checking Ed25519 QR signatures, and escalating syndicate anomalies.
- **Secondary Users (Checkers)**: Senior Bureau Directors and Regional Officers exercising statutory oversight and Maker-Checker dual control. Their primary job is reviewing high-exposure maturity horizons, authorizing or rejecting deposit release requests under AICTE APH Clause 6.46, and supervising cryptographic audit integrity.
- **External Stakeholders**: Technical Institution Administrators submitting mandatory security deposit instruments and responding to deficiency notices; Bank Nodal Officers confirming legacy paper receipts via tokenized one-time links.

## Product Purpose

Prama (प्रमा — uncontradicted, verified truth in Indian epistemology) transforms statutory Fixed Deposit Receipt (FDR) monitoring for the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) from passive, fragmented document archiving into an active cryptographic trust terminal, forensic discrepancy engine, and regulatory risk intelligence cockpit. Success means zero undetected counterfeit or spliced deposits, rapid 12ms offline authenticity verification, automated discovery of cross-state syndicate re-use, and continuous protection of student and institutional statutory funds.

- Enforces a deterministic **Four-Tier Provenance Matrix** (`Self-Declared` → `Doc-Extracted` → `Cross-Validated` → `Bank-Confirmed`) so unverified applicant claims are never mistaken for validated truth.
- Provides **12ms offline cryptographic verification** via bank-signed Ed25519 QR envelopes, eliminating reliance on non-existent or fragile public core-banking APIs.
- Employs **Hardware-accelerated Vector Graph Topology** (`@xyflow/react`) to reveal hidden multi-institution collusion and re-used instrument serials.
- Features a **Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Merkle Ledger** with live backdoor sabotage detection to prove database integrity to regulatory juries and auditors.
- Implements an **Open Knowledge Format (OKF)** dynamic policy engine and PageIndex Copilot with verbatim AICTE handbook citations and strict AST-enforced decision isolation (AI models never approve or reject regulatory decisions).

## Product Principles

1. **Mathematical Truth Over Clerical Trust**: Every document claim must be corroborated by cryptographic proof (Ed25519 / SHA-256) or deterministic cross-validation; unverified assertions remain strictly labeled as Self-Declared.
2. **Deterministic Decision Authority**: AI and language models assist with search and natural language synthesis, but never possess the authority to approve, reject, or certify regulatory status.
3. **Forensic Transparency & Non-Repudiation**: Administrative decisions and system actions are sealed in an append-only cryptographic ledger; direct database tampering is immediately exposed.
4. **Dense, Operational Clarity**: Scrutiny officers require maximum situational awareness, actionable discrepancy alerts, and synchronized forensic views rather than decorative whitespace.
5. **Proactive Lifecycle Governance**: Statutory protection requires forward-looking risk intelligence (maturity cliff forecasting and automated escalation ladders) before deposits lapse unmonitored.
